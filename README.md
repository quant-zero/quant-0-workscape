Quant-0

quant-0 is the coordination layer for AI agents.

Quant-0 brings together unified model access, autonomous agents, multi-agent crews, workflows, and a permissionless AI marketplace into one open platform built for the next generation of the AI ecosystem.

While powerful protocols like MCP make AI integrations possible, setting them up can still be difficult for most users. Quant-0 removes that complexity by running the infrastructure for you, so developers and everyday users alike can focus on building, automating, and collaborating with AI instead of managing tooling and configuration.

What Quant-0 Enables
Unified access to multiple AI models
Autonomous AI agents that can reason and act
Multi-agent crews that collaborate on tasks
Workflow orchestration for complex automation
Permissionless integrations through open standards
An open AI marketplace for tools, agents, and services
Seamless MCP-powered infrastructure without setup headaches
Vision

We believe AI should be open, composable, and accessible.

Quant-0 is designed to become the operating layer where agents, tools, workflows, and users connect together in a unified ecosystem built for the future of AI coordination.
